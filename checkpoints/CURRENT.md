For trains it compares the feed's arrival time and the blended time that moves the drawn train (`blendStops`) with when the train reports standing at the stop. A capped sample goes to `raw.transit_prediction_scores` (400 rows a system a run, 21 days) and three small views are refreshed after each run. Two endpoints serve them: `/api/transit-health/coverage` (stretches with learned times and samples per line or route, schedule and time band) and `/api/transit-health/accuracy` (median error and share within a minute, with learned times and without). A 7-minute dry run on 2026-11-14 scored 353 train predictions (median 25 s off with learned times, 27 s with the feed alone) and 45 bus predictions (39 s off by default, and late more often than early). Seen on the way: a bus line's shape carries the stops on both kerbs, so the map's bus motion stops at stops the bus does not serve, and only 29 of M22's 102 learned stretches join stops that are neighbours on its shape. Every chart names its endpoint and load time. The page is its own 5.8 kB chunk; the boot chunk's budget went from 294 to 294.5 kB for the Settings row and its label in eight languages. Both parts are live since 2026-11-15: the first run stored 740 scores (trains a median 30 s off with learned times and 32 s with the feed alone; buses 72 s and about a minute late). On the live page the 1 train's stretches showed both directions in one list, because the feed gives that line one direction number both ways; the page now reads a train's direction from its platform id (branch `fix/11-line-direction`). Each score now also stores the conditions at the time (branch `feat/11-score-conditions`; owner, 2026-11-14: "traffic, time of day, weather etc"): whether a notice was in effect for the line, rain or snow from the app's weather answer (one reading for the city per run), and for a bus the DOT speed link within 80 m of the stop. A backtest on 398,216 subway observations from 2026-11-12 to 11-15 found the band pool, the single hour and an adaptive rule equally good (median 8 s off each); only the L gains from the hour, so the estimator is unchanged. The MTA publishes bus travel times between timepoints by route, day and hour (data.ny.gov `kufs-yh3x`, 10.7 million rows from 2025-01; Open NY's terms allow any lawful reuse with no attribution).
